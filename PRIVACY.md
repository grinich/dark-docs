# Dark Docs privacy policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Dark Docs changes the on-screen appearance of Google Docs. It is maintained by Michael Grinich.

## Information used on your device

Dark Docs reads your browser's light/dark appearance preference and saves two settings in the extension's local Chrome storage: your appearance choice (System, Dark, or Light) and whether document pages should appear dark. These settings remain in that Chrome profile; Dark Docs does not use Chrome's synchronized storage.

The extension applies styles to Google Docs pages and its embedded sharing interface. It changes a small number of appearance attributes on the page. It does not read or collect document text, titles, comments, identities, email addresses, browsing history, cookies, or account credentials. It does not change the contents of your documents.

## Collection, sharing, and tracking

Dark Docs does not transmit data to its developer or any third party. It has no server, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or external code downloads. It does not sell user data or use it for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending decisions.

Google Docs and other Google services continue to operate under Google's own policies. The extension does not alter those services' data practices.

## Retention and your choices

You can change the saved preferences in the extension popup. They remain stored locally until replaced or until you uninstall the extension. Uninstalling Dark Docs removes its extension storage. There is no developer-held copy of these preferences to delete.

## Limited use

Dark Docs uses local appearance information only to provide its stated theming feature and follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. It does not use Google APIs to obtain account or document data.

## Contact and updates

For questions, use the support contact shown on the Dark Docs Chrome Web Store listing. If you choose to contact support or open a GitHub issue, information you include is handled by that service and may be visible to others; avoid including private document text or account details. This policy will be updated when the extension's data practices change.
